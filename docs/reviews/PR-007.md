## Optimization and proof boundaries

- For fixed map demand and baseline, output pressure is inversely proportional to `1 + total modeled gear Damage`. Survival is an independent representative-hit ratio. Demand and capacity formulas are unchanged.
- The search chooses one legal option per slot. Each priced option includes every prerequisite star between the worn or acquired starting state and the final item. Costs are integer sums of rounded individual prices. Requirements are remaining qualifying pieces per protected tier. A sufficient higher threshold preserves lower required effects on the same tier. Unmodeled combat effects remain protected by the default contract.
- The unrestricted pass either minimizes star gold at or below the pressure target or minimizes cost within the existing anchored tolerance of the exact best priced output when the target cannot be reached with comparable prices. Gold ties minimize replacements and then fills using the existing acquisition rank. Remaining ties use deterministic native slot and candidate ordering.
- A second pass runs with a specified budget when the first answer is over budget, short of target, or incomplete. It prunes over-budget branches and minimizes star gold if any qualifying result is found. Otherwise it maximizes comparable damage, then minimizes gold and acquisition rank for equal modeled output. The existing exact set-aware suffix Damage maximum bounds any remaining completion. A separate optimistic relaxation gives each remaining slot its best zero-cost output and values any remaining spend at the highest available incremental rate. Taking the smaller of the two upper bounds is admissible. The rate is a search bound, with no item ranking exposed to the player.
- The two enumeration passes each have a 150,000-node bound. A cutoff withholds optimality. A known legal unpriced choice is included in the unrestricted output ceiling, where it can prevent an unreachability proof. Unverified acquisitions prevent completion claims beyond verified candidates. Conditional drops and estimated prices remain visible, including when calculable star gold fits the chosen limit. A positive unrestricted minimum is shown as established only after completion without excluded unknown prices or unverified drops. Each price is scoped to stars and leaves farming effort and uncertain ascension out of the quote.
- The execution guide tries higher early modeled gains first, then uses a deterministic slot-mask search to find a sequence that maintains the original protected thresholds. Counts depend only on which slots are finished. Equipping at zero stars can transiently lose a piece, so the check requires a valid count at that intermediate state. Grouping sequential monotonic star gains on one item preserves the existence of a safe equip-first route. The shared evaluator supplies the displayed intermediate results. The guide reports unavoidable loss within this execution model and does not prove the order optimal for gameplay timing. Off-character upgrades and inherited stars are unverified.
- Changing map, tier, baseline, policy or equipment invalidates both searches and complete comparisons through the context owner. A selection or explicit simulator load reuses completed results. An edited draft is independent of those planning controls and of same-world equipment refreshes. Context changes fence the prior draft and armor. Work is incremental with a profiler-based time slice when available, pauses off the planner tab and releases unfinished results on close. Hovering never starts a search.
