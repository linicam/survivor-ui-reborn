## Optimization and proof boundaries

- For fixed map demand and baseline, output pressure is inversely proportional to `1 + total modeled gear Damage`. Survival is an independent representative-hit ratio. Demand and capacity formulas are unchanged.
- The search chooses one legal option per slot. Each priced option includes every prerequisite star between the worn or acquired starting state and the final item. Costs are integer sums of rounded individual prices. Requirements are remaining qualifying pieces per protected tier. A sufficient higher threshold preserves lower required effects on the same tier. Unmodeled combat effects remain protected by the default contract.
- The unrestricted pass either minimizes star gold at or below the pressure target or minimizes cost within the existing anchored tolerance of the exact best priced output when the target cannot be reached with comparable prices. Gold ties minimize replacements and then fills using the existing acquisition rank. Remaining ties use deterministic native slot and candidate ordering.
- A second pass runs with a specified budget when the first answer is over budget, short of target, or incomplete. It prunes over-budget branches and minimizes star gold if any qualifying result is found. Otherwise it maximizes comparable damage, then minimizes gold and acquisition rank for equal modeled output. The existing exact set-aware suffix Damage maximum bounds any remaining completion. A separate optimistic relaxation gives each remaining slot its best zero-cost output and values any remaining spend at the highest available incremental rate. Taking the smaller of the two upper bounds is admissible. The rate is a search bound, with no item ranking exposed to the player.
- The two enumeration passes each have a 150,000-node bound. A cutoff withholds optimality. A known legal unpriced choice is included in the unrestricted output ceiling, where it can prevent an unreachability proof. Unverified acquisitions prevent completion claims beyond verified candidates. Conditional drops and estimated prices remain visible, including when calculable star gold fits the chosen limit. A positive unrestricted minimum is shown as established only after completion without excluded unknown prices or unverified drops. Each price is scoped to stars and leaves farming effort and uncertain ascension out of the quote.
- The execution guide tries higher early modeled gains first, then uses a deterministic slot-mask search to find a sequence that maintains the original protected thresholds. Counts depend only on which slots are finished. Equipping at zero stars can transiently lose a piece, so the check requires a valid count at that intermediate state. Grouping sequential monotonic star gains on one item preserves the existence of a safe equip-first route. The shared evaluator supplies the displayed intermediate results. The guide reports unavoidable loss within this execution model and does not prove the order optimal for gameplay timing. Off-character upgrades and inherited stars are unverified.
- Changing map, tier, baseline, policy or equipment invalidates both searches and complete comparisons through the context owner. A selection or explicit simulator load reuses completed results. An edited draft is independent of those planning controls and of same-world equipment refreshes. Context changes fence the prior draft and armor. Work is incremental with a profiler-based time slice when available, pauses off the planner tab and releases unfinished results on close. Hovering never starts a search.

### Algorithms, result semantics, and lifecycle

- Candidate generation uses registered class slots and tier legality. A zero-star floor exists for verified drops or empty slots, and a worn option remains for every occupied slot. A candidate with any unknown prerequisite price cannot enter the priced lists or the budget incumbent. Conditional zero-star drops are eligible for zero modeled gold and remain conditional in presentation. The deterministic acquisition rank puts any worn replacement after all possible fills.
- Same-tier dominance is safe for the current objective because it preserves the slot, protection credit and acquisition rank, improves or ties damage, and costs no more. The suffix memo keys encode unmet set thresholds. It returns an upper bound over admissible suffixes. Gold-rate pruning relaxes the set constraints, lets every remaining slot realize its independent zero-cost best, and applies the highest positive incremental rate to every remaining gold unit. This can overestimate output. Reusing the corrected pressure inverse in both passes makes the bounds conservative at the snap.
- Unrestricted Economy retains its minimum-gold objective for qualifying configurations and its anchored tolerance for unreachable targets. The budget pass gives priority to qualification, then gold and acquisition rank. For a non-qualifying incumbent it lowers pressure first, then applies the same tie order. The passes can rank the same priced leaves differently, so the budget incumbent considers scored leaves even when the unrestricted tolerance chooses a different final representative. Proof flags depend on completion and availability evidence. The 150,000-node limits remain in force.
- Minimum target quote, budget shortfall, modeled budget left and Max Gear refer to separate fields. The simulator loads the selected configuration rather than rebuilding it from the quote. An omitted budget and zero gold exercise distinct paths. Known legal unpriced choices can block a reachability proof, and an unverified drop excludes a broader proof. Account gold stays unavailable with manual budget entry.
- Execution uses equip-at-zero followed by ascending upgrades for a replacement. The selected target carries no investment on its predecessor. An intermediate star on the same item can only add qualifying set credit under the current model. Advancing later stars immediately after the same equip is therefore safe for the existence of a preserving sequence. Each slot mask fixes the counts, including separate tier requirements, so a failed mask can be safely memoized. The heuristic ordering, cumulative known subtotal, estimated or unknown terms, and the scope of unavoidable loss within the equip-first model are retained. Recorded and synthetic execution rechecks cover exact final pieces, four-piece transitions, eight-piece and empowerment preservation, conflicting tiers, and displayed unknown quote terms. Alternative real-game acquisition routes lack evidence.
- World entry clears the prior wearer, drafts and pending searches. A changed normalized native zone or class invalidates the old armor. Identical decoded snapshots preserve it. Explicit Hub refresh waits for an `AZSZONE` before accepting subsequent armor. A manual Dalaran target supplies only preview demand and its explicit baseline origin. The native Campaign map has precedence. Missing equipment, map-specific difficulty caps, legacy baselines, same-world draft preservation and settings cancellation are covered in the combined widget test. The raw armor event still has no run ID, so a delayed older snapshot arriving after a new valid zone can be indistinguishable.
- The existing 465×420 zhCN fixture exercises both independent scroll regions, pressure and price text, and refresh ordering. The focus regression exercises invalid blur and Escape, and the distinct tooltip paths check intrinsic stats and ownership. Planned-item hover performs bounded per-item evaluation. Simulator and native previews use their respective loadouts. Neither hover path schedules optimization. Client glyph metrics and keyboard interception remain native verification items. Keep the established two-pane layout and consolidated acceptance checklist above.
