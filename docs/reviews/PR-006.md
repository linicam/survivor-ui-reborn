## A. Implementation summary

The previous Dalaran view reused the last Campaign run, shared a map baseline across classes, and could fill the baseline automatically. Upgrade plans ranked a single listed damage attribute. Advanced commands exposed manual gear, quote, and map entry.

The new planner consumes the current world's `AZSZONE` and `AZSARMOR` events. A player explicitly saves the actual loadout and map as a class-specific 100% baseline. The planner shows separate output and survival pressures and uses the larger value for its search objective. Plan A includes eligible current-expansion drops as conditional replacements. Plan B keeps equipped pieces and tiers and considers star upgrades. Both plans can populate the independent Equipment Simulator. The native character panel retains its **Upgrades** entry and appends single-item comparisons on hover. The planner also opens through `/sui gear` or SurvivorUI Global settings.

The implementation preserves the native gear formula, the checked per-star price calculation, the public map coefficients, set thresholds, and the native character panel post-hook. Existing card, tracker, and diagnostic features stay on their own paths. The normal workflow drops manual equipment and price editing, old snapshots as fallbacks, refresh controls, manual map selection, and the corresponding old slash commands. Legacy settings are kept on disk. Only the explicit save action writes the new baseline.

Delivery is partial. The supported workflow is implemented, with a live Dalaran sample still needed to verify that native messages identify the selected Campaign map and difficulty. Account ascension inputs remain unavailable through the inspected World interface. The next-tier preview evaluates a zero-star drop. Game research and continuous data collection remain separate from this task.

## C. Formulas, assumptions, and evidence

| Quantity | Calculation or rule | Evidence type and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Piece attribute | `(slot base × tier slot scale + class profile base × tier profile scale) × common factor × (1 + min(stars, steps) × star_step + top-star bonus)` | Public gear formula and installed `AZS_GEN.pieces` and `gearTiers` definitions. Flat health and regeneration are absolute units. Percentage attribute differences are percentage points. The model sums before rounding. Native item display can round individual pieces. |
| Incremental star price | `floor(target star base × tier multiplier + 0.5)` | Derived from the checked November 16, 2026 price schedule. Bases for stars 1–5 are `408, 816, 1496, 2720, 4080` and already include the reduction. Multiple steps sum individually rounded quotes. Weapon parity was user-confirmed. Native factors cross-check Exotic `5.8`, Mythic `7.2`, and Elementium `7.6`. Exotic, Elementium, and the fifth Legendary star are formula estimates. |
| Set thresholds | Four matching armor pieces at 1+ stars, eight at 3+ stars, and eligible effects empowered with eight pieces at 5 stars | Native `setRules` and `setBonuses`. Verified numerical gold, pickup, Echo, and critical-damage modifiers contribute to attribute totals. Conditional, skill-specific, or unresolved effects remain qualitative. |
| Map output demand | `health_factor × Campaign tier health multiplier` | Public zone data and previously checked standard Campaign scaling. Normal through Heroic V health multipliers are `1, 1.79, 1.97, 2.16, 2.45, 2.72`. |
| Map survival demand | `first boss base hit × Campaign tier hit multiplier` | Public boss data and previously checked standard Campaign scaling. Hit multipliers are `1, 1.65, 1.77, 1.88, 2.01, 2.16`. The first boss hit is a representative early hit. |
| Output capability | `1 + sum of gear damage fractions` | Modeling assumption. The reference ability receives ordinary gear damage and non-gear effects are held neutral. |
| Survival capability | `(class starting HP + flat gear HP) / (1 − min(gear reduction, 0.60))` | Derived effective-health proxy using class data and the standard mitigation cap. Non-equipment modifiers and cap exceptions such as Warrior Titan are outside the proxy. |
| Normalized pressure | `100 × target demand / baseline demand × baseline capability / target capability` | Derived independently for output and survival. Values within `0.000001` percentage points of 100% normalize to 100%. |
| Combined difficulty | `max(output pressure, survival pressure)` when both exist | Product decision that preserves the limiting dimension. A partial estimate remains separate. |

The pressure estimates describe early equipment demand. They have no empirical interpretation as clear probability or overall DPS. They exclude enemy density, encounter scripts, affixes, challenge scaling, talents, critical hits, cooldowns, Echo, regeneration, movement, summons, and growth routes from the pressure conversion. The public map balance snapshot used for this implementation is `21071e86fee66ca7`. ## D. Optimization correctness and product decisions

Plan A enumerates known Campaign drops in the target expansion using the zone-pair rule, explicit overrides, and the native extra tiers for the expansion's final pair of maps. The candidate space includes upgrades to already worn pieces, including an actual item the player brought from another expansion. Replacement costs show calculable stars from the zero-star drop state and list acquisition separately. Plan B holds the slot and quality of each equipped item fixed. The account gold balance is outside both candidate filters. The simulator supports hypothetical choices outside either plan and distinguishes unknown acquisition costs from calculable stars. Downgrades have no assumed refund.

Incremental depth-first search seeds each plan with the actual setup and checks the highest quoted same-tier setup as an initial improvement. A current estimate at or below 100% returns the actual configuration for zero gold. A feasible improvement minimizes comparable gold at or below 100%. When no candidate meets that target, the search minimizes remaining combined pressure and uses gold as the secondary criterion. Equal candidates keep the deterministic first result. After the seed, traversal follows native slot order and sorts choices by cost, tier rank, and stars.

Pruning uses nonnegative accumulated cost, remaining slot counts that could preserve active set thresholds, and optimistic suffix caps on gear damage, health, and armor contributions. Taking the best remaining value for each attribute independently can combine incompatible items. The resulting capability remains an optimistic bound. Quantified set effects leave the three search inputs unchanged. The solver explicitly preserves current set thresholds with unquantified combat effects. Self-review caught and fixed an incorrect cutoff after finding a feasible result. The search now keeps testing cheaper candidates against the 100% threshold.

Each plan visits at most 150,000 nodes. Completed searches establish the optimum only for included and comparable candidates. When a budget cutoff occurs, the UI labels the result as the best found and leaves unreachable status unproven. When prices are missing, the UI distinguishes what the priced options establish from what unpriced candidates might achieve. The test oracles independently enumerated 80 stars-only and two mixed-tier cases. Synthetic examples cover a 100% reference at zero gold, a conditional zero-star Exotic replacement that meets the target where stars-only cannot, and mixed-tier solutions that preserve an active four-piece bonus. Tests also repeat the mixed-tier searches to check deterministic results.

## E. Native API investigation and unresolved limitations

1. **Selected Campaign map in Dalaran.** The previously observed client displayed nine equipped pieces in Dalaran. The user reported that Dalaran preserves the lobby selection. The public API documents `zone`, `dt`, and `heroic` in `AZSZONE`. Installed `Run.lua` replaces `AZS_ZONE` from those fields, and the inspected `Hub.lua` exposed no independent snapshot for the lobby's selection. The remaining uncertainty is whether the Dalaran World message carries the selected map key and difficulty or only reports Dalaran. The latter leaves the map comparison unavailable and still allows received gear to be displayed. The minimum evidence is the first relevant message after selecting an identifiable Campaign map at a non-Normal tier. [Addon API](https://azreborn.com/guides/addon-api)
2. **Direct ascension and collection.** The hero menu is in a separate Glue Lua environment. World `AZSARMOR` in `Units.lua` only describes equipped slots. Proving Ground equipment exposed through `AZS_PG` represents a practice setup. It cannot establish the live collection or completed maps. The next-expansion final-map rule for Legendary and higher ascension was reported by the user and lacks independent server verification here. Direct ascension awaits readable account eligibility, a reliable quote, and the resulting star-state contract. The next-tier preview therefore uses a hypothetical zero-star drop with a separate acquisition explanation. [Gear progression](https://azreborn.com/guides/progression-and-cosmetics#gear-stars-and-sets)
3. **Empty equipment.** The native addon notes that a fully unequipped hero may receive no `AZSARMOR` message. Explicit empty data is valid. Silence keeps the state waiting and supplies no basis for inferring an empty loadout. When equipment is reported, the planner assumes it represents the complete worn list.
4. **Catalog and model coverage.** Weapon slot and icon coverage follows observed items or available native class and tier definitions. Unconfirmed second weapon slots are omitted. Special set and skill interactions remain qualitative. The planner holds active unquantified combat set thresholds by default.
5. **Native tooltip.** `Inspect.lua` establishes the order of `RefreshDoll`, named slot textures and their parent frames, native `OnEnter`, and `GameTooltip` ownership. The selected approach appends to that tooltip using the existing post-hook. Simulator and plan icons also expose the read-only comparison. Available evidence has not established a need for a companion-tooltip fallback. Real placement and text wrapping remain pending. [Addon API](https://azreborn.com/guides/addon-api)

The historical screenshot, price material, installed third-party source and generated data, and the offline comparison script used with those installed data remain local. This report records their relevant observations, contracts, calculations, and measured results. Independent reviewers can inspect the public references, SurvivorUI source, tests, replay fixtures, and CI evidence. The local observations should be treated as reported evidence until independently reproduced or verified.

## F. Performance measurements

The Windows offline benchmark used Lupa Lua 5.1 and the installed Reborn 0.7.4 item catalog. The Paladin and Warlock each had nine one-star Legendary pieces, a Naxxramas Normal baseline, and a Shadowmoon Valley Heroic III target. Each class ran 300 attribute evaluations and 300 single-piece simulator evaluations. Median attribute evaluation was approximately 0.028 ms. Replacement, pressure recalculation, and quoting took 0.035–0.040 ms at the median. Initialization took about 0.29–1.47 ms, with the larger candidate construction in Plan A.

| Sample | Visited nodes | Median batch of 240 nodes | Total wall time across batches | Search result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Paladin A | 150,000 | 0.307 ms | 221.9 ms | Cutoff at 421.588%, 86,768 estimated star gold |
| Paladin B | 97,686 | 0.341 ms | 162.4 ms | Complete at 422.355%, 125,748 gold |
| Warlock A | 150,000 | 0.298 ms | 212.4 ms | Cutoff at 395.884%, 165,648 estimated star gold |
| Warlock B | 101,406 | 0.358 ms | 173.2 ms | Complete at 422.355%, 125,748 gold |

The unpruned benchmark space had 35 options per slot in Plan A and five in Plan B. The live UI schedules at most 240 nodes per plan per frame and checks a shared 1.25 ms soft time budget when a clock is available. Without it, the node budget remains. The longest offline batch was approximately 1.86 ms. These timings exclude actual client rendering and do not measure frame stalls.

Only the current context's two results are cached. Changing worlds invalidates them. Hiding the view stops work and releases unfinished searches. The simulator tab pauses the search and hovering evaluates only adjacent equipment states. Disabling the feature unregisters the world-entry listener. Existing `AZS.On` callbacks early-return because that native interface exposes registration only. Candidates are organized by slot, search depth is bounded by up to 11 slots, and UI rows are reused. Memory usage was not measured.
