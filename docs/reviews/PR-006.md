## A. Implementation summary

The previous Dalaran view reused the last Campaign run, shared a map baseline across classes, and could fill the baseline automatically. Upgrade plans ranked a single listed damage attribute. Advanced commands exposed manual gear, quote, and map entry.

The new planner consumes the current world's `AZSZONE` and `AZSARMOR` events. A player explicitly saves the actual loadout and map as a class-specific 100% baseline. The planner shows separate output and survival pressures and uses output pressure as its only numeric search objective. Plan A includes eligible current-expansion drops as conditional replacements. Plan B keeps equipped pieces and tiers and considers star upgrades. Both plans can populate the independent Equipment Simulator. The native character panel retains its **Upgrades** entry and appends single-item comparisons on hover. The planner also opens through `/sui gear` or SurvivorUI Global settings.

The implementation preserves the native gear formula, the checked per-star price calculation, the public map coefficients, set thresholds, and the native character panel post-hook. Existing card, tracker, and diagnostic features stay on their own paths. The normal workflow drops manual equipment and price editing, old snapshots as fallbacks, refresh controls, manual map selection, and the corresponding old slash commands. Legacy settings are kept on disk. Only the explicit save action writes the new baseline.

The independent runtime checks confirm that a live Campaign supplies map, tier, and equipment together, while Dalaran supplies current equipment and its own hub zone. A Campaign therefore offers the full workflow when the player saves a comparable class baseline. Dalaran opens directly into the simulator and keeps equipment attributes, gold quotes, set changes, and single-item previews available. The Campaign planner, baseline action, unavailable pressure rows, and plan controls are suppressed in the hub. Account ascension inputs remain unavailable through the inspected World interface, so the next-tier preview evaluates a zero-star drop. Game research and continuous data collection remain separate.

The correction round makes the theoretical scope visible, reevaluates saved inputs across compatible changes, separates known mode restrictions from affix qualifications, and freezes the zone context at its event boundary. Actual equipment, candidate generation, simulation, and tooltips share one class/slot/tier/star contract. The search uses legal seeds and stronger bounds within the original node budget. Simulator and plan icons now compare their own equipment contexts.

The R11–R14 correction removed lower-quality worn replacements from Plan A and prefers retaining worn items and avoiding newly filled slots when the modeled objective and comparable gold tie. Lower-quality equipment stays available in the manual simulator. The interface identifies combined and component pressure, explains the limiting dimension, and applies a common sign and color convention to gear and pressure changes. The pressure formula remains the existing three-stat proxy after the combat-stat investigation.

The planner and simulator now show equipment beside its effect on the complete setup. The planner pins context, baseline, current pressure, and both plan summaries. The selected plan directly supplies the equipment list and full effects panel. The simulator uses the same effects panel while editing its own draft. Its rows surface worn-item comparisons, gold and acquisition notes only when they differ. A compact breakpoint stacks independent equipment and results panes for narrow viewports. This layout is retained for R15–R17. Formula inputs, quotes, baseline storage, and native acquisition contracts remain the same.

## C. Formulas, assumptions, and evidence

| Quantity | Calculation or rule | Evidence type and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Piece attribute | `(slot base × tier slot scale + class profile base × tier profile scale) × common factor × (1 + min(stars, steps) × star_step + top-star bonus)` | Public gear formula and installed `AZS_GEN.pieces` and `gearTiers` definitions. Flat health and regeneration are absolute units. Percentage attribute differences are percentage points. The model sums before rounding. Native item display can round individual pieces. |
| Incremental star price | `floor(target star base × tier multiplier + 0.5)` | Derived from the checked November 16, 2026 price schedule. | Set thresholds | Four matching armor pieces at 1+ stars, eight at 3+ stars, and eligible effects empowered with eight pieces at 5 stars | Native `setRules` and `setBonuses`. | Map output demand | `health_factor × Campaign tier health multiplier` | Public zone data and previously checked standard Campaign scaling. Normal through Heroic V health multipliers are `1, 1.79, 1.97, 2.16, 2.45, 2.72`. |
| Map survival demand | `first boss base hit × Campaign tier hit multiplier` | Public boss data and previously checked standard Campaign scaling. Hit multipliers are `1, 1.65, 1.77, 1.88, 2.01, 2.16`. The first boss hit is a representative early hit. |
| Output capability | `1 + sum of gear damage fractions` | Modeling assumption. The reference ability receives ordinary gear damage and non-gear effects are held neutral. |

## D. Optimization correctness and product decisions

Plan A enumerates known Campaign drops in the target expansion using the zone-pair rule, explicit overrides, and the native extra tiers for the expansion's final pair of maps. The candidate space includes upgrades to already worn pieces, including an actual item the player brought from another expansion, plus strictly higher-quality drops for occupied slots and valid drops for empty slots. Lower-quality worn replacements never enter the search, including its seeds. Replacement costs show calculable stars from the zero-star drop state and list acquisition separately. Plan B holds the slot and quality of each equipped item fixed. The account gold balance is outside both candidate filters. The simulator supports hypothetical choices outside either plan and distinguishes unknown acquisition costs from calculable stars. Downgrades have no assumed refund.

The two plans have the same output target. A current setup at or below 100% output returns the actual gear at zero gold. For reachable targets the priced objective is minimum known gold, then fewer worn replacements, then fewer added slots. For unreachable priced targets Economy first reaches the exact maximum modeled Damage within the existing pressure tolerance and then minimizes the same gold and acquisition tuple. The existing deterministic slot and option order settles any remaining ties. Survival, HP and Damage Reduction do not participate in candidate dominance, bounds or acceptance.

Each candidate retains its legal rarity, stars, drop evidence and contribution to a protected set. Same-slot, same-tier dominance compares only additive Damage and known gold within identical protection groups, which also share acquisition rank. Calculated set modifiers add no ordinary Damage to the current output model. The live search uses a memoized suffix recurrence keyed by ordered slot position and outstanding set-threshold counts. At each state it consumes at most one protected tier requirement per item and takes the maximum additive Damage over all legal continuations. This gives an exact priced output ceiling and a legal incumbent. Ties at minimum output pressure are anchored to this ceiling, with a conservative Damage guard for sum-order roundoff.

The cost pass visits at most 150,000 nodes, retains the 240-step batch and 1.25 ms soft slice, and starts with the exact priced-capacity seed. It prunes branches that cannot reach the selected output bound or cannot improve the incumbent's gold/acquisition tuple. Its optimistic gold bound subtracts free per-slot Damage and applies the best gain per gold in the remaining slots to the deficit. It relaxes set constraints and therefore cannot overestimate needed gold. Set-state work yields per uncached state. Without set conflicts Max Gear takes the highest verified quality and native maximum stars per slot directly. With conflicts it considers only the slot's maximum and protected-tier alternatives at maximum stars. It minimizes the number of necessary lower-tier holds, then maximizes modeled Damage among those assignments. Exact Max Gear ties follow native slot and candidate order. It preserves an actual higher quality and fills only verified slots for Plan A. Plan B maxes stars on worn pieces, with no replacements or new slots.

The proof for a completed unreachable search uses the exact output ceiling over known legal candidates, including candidates with unknown prices. Labels remain scoped to modeled output and verified acquisition. If a legal unpriced path reaches the threshold, a price optimum across that path is unavailable even when the priced search is complete. Incomplete acquisition evidence or a budget cutoff prevents an unreachable declaration. If a priced incumbent reaches the target before cutoff, the UI reports that configuration with the price optimum still unproven. Known quote subtotals and missing price portions remain distinguishable.

### R11 tie handling and reproduction

This subsection records the historical R11 comparison before R15–R17. R11 preserved feasibility, pressure and comparable gold as its primary objectives. When these tie, it minimizes worn replacements, then newly filled slots. A rank of `replacements × (valid slot count + 1) + fills` implements the lexicographic tuple without pricing a drop in gold. Incumbents at actual equipment, max worn stars, full-search seed, seed reduction and leaves use this order where applicable. Seeds are legal candidates. Greedy seeding uses optimistic pressure bounds and consults candidate order for pressure ties. Same-tier dominance cannot increase acquisition rank within a slot and still requires equal protected-set contribution. Capability and price bounds remain optimistic over the smaller candidate domain. Branches at equal gold remain searchable if their accumulated acquisition rank could improve the incumbent. Extra stars, gold and acquisition rank are nonnegative along a path. The existing optimistic root pressure tie range is unchanged. The search does not convert survival improvements to a tie-break priority when output controls the combined metric.

## E. Native API investigation and unresolved limitations

1. **World map and tier.** The public World API documents `zone`, `dt`, and `heroic` in `AZSZONE`. Installed `Run.lua` replaces `AZS_ZONE` using the native parser. The World `Hub.lua` account handler covers hub interactions. The inspected API and Lua do not expose a dedicated query for lobby selection. The documented read-only `sync` resends the World state. See the two runtime observations below for the distinction between a Campaign and the Hub. [Addon API](https://azreborn.com/guides/addon-api)
2. **Direct ascension and collection.** The hero menu is in a separate Glue Lua environment. World `AZSARMOR` in `Units.lua` only describes equipped slots. Proving Ground equipment exposed through `AZS_PG` represents a practice setup. It cannot establish the live collection or completed maps. The next-expansion final-map rule for Legendary and higher ascension was reported by the user and lacks independent server verification here. Direct ascension awaits readable account eligibility, a reliable quote, and the resulting star-state contract. The next-tier preview therefore uses a hypothetical zero-star drop with a separate acquisition explanation. [Gear progression](https://azreborn.com/guides/progression-and-cosmetics#gear-stars-and-sets)
3. **Empty equipment.** The native addon notes that a fully unequipped hero may receive no `AZSARMOR` message. Explicit empty data is valid. Silence keeps the state waiting and supplies no basis for inferring an empty loadout. When equipment is reported, the planner assumes it represents the complete worn list.
4. **Catalog and model coverage.** Class weapon layout uses native layout metadata where available, then native weapon lists and the established stock layout. Actual data cannot expand the class's slot whitelist. A class without a pack-tier weapon override uses the documented Legendary appearance fallback. An explicit incomplete per-tier weapon list provides insufficient evidence for that slot's drop, so Plan A excludes it. A class-valid simulator configuration can still show its theoretical benefit. Special set and skill interactions remain qualitative. [Content reference](https://azreborn.com/guides/content-reference)
5. **Native tooltip.** `Inspect.lua` establishes the order of `RefreshDoll`, named slot textures and their parent frames, native `OnEnter`, and `GameTooltip` ownership. The selected approach appends to that tooltip using the existing post-hook. Simulator and plan icons also expose the read-only comparison. Available evidence has not established a need for a companion-tooltip fallback. Real placement and text wrapping remain pending. [Addon API](https://azreborn.com/guides/addon-api)

6. **Modifiers and encounter identity.** The documented `affixes` feed is a comma-separated names list. Equivalent name lists are normalized as values. The legacy `aff` signal alone is not evidence of an active named modifier. Heroic tiers add built-in rules. The UI identifies the baseline, target, or both when the comparison omits extra rules. Explicit unsupported modes suppress the pressure and plans. The standard Campaign estimate excludes Rift and Endless encounters. The documented feed lacks reliable mode identity for every context. [Addon API](https://azreborn.com/guides/addon-api) [Heroic tiers and modes](https://azreborn.com/guides/runs-and-zones#difficulty-tiers)
7. **Session identity.** The analyzer stores normalized context values independently of native tables and invalidates gear and running searches when those fields change. It accepts armor only following the current zone boundary. `AZSARMOR` has no run identifier. An older armor message delivered after a new valid zone message remains indistinguishable if it otherwise validates.

The historical screenshot, price material, installed third-party source and generated data, and the independent expected-value CSV remain local. Reviewers can inspect the public references, SurvivorUI source, [parameterized verification script](../../scripts/verify_reborn_gear.py), tests, replay fixtures, and CI. The [input format and reproduction commands](../REBORN_GEAR.md#offline-verification) document verification with independently provided data. Treat earlier local observations as reported evidence until reproduced.
